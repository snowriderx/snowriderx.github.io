Snow Rider 3D Privacy Policy

At Snow Rider 3D, your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used when you play the official browser version developed by GemGamer Studio.

1. Data Collection

Snow Rider 3D does not require players to register or provide personal details. We may collect basic non-personal information such as browser type, device type, and gameplay statistics to improve performance.

2. Cookies

The game may use cookies to remember session preferences and track gameplay activity. These cookies do not collect personal data and are only used to enhance user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to better understand player behavior. These services collect anonymous data but never personal identifiers.

4. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited data we collect. However, as a browser-based game, we recommend players ensure their own device security when accessing online content.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@snowriderx.github.io

Snow Rider 3D is developed and published by GemGamer Studio – official browser version.
